Description : 

You have to read two numbers from user and you have to swap the two number.

Sample Execution : 

Test case 1 : 

Enter the number1 : 10

Enter the number2 : 20

Before swap :

num1 is 10

num2 is 20

After swap :

num1 is 20

num2 is 10

Test case 2 :


Enter the number1 : 5

Enter the number2 : 10

Before swap :

num1 is 5

num2 is 10

After swap :

num1 is 10

num2 is 5
